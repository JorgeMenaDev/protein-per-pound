## Instructions

1. Boil and mash the sweet potato.
2. Put two tablespoons of oats in a blender and blend it to make a powder.
3. Mix the oats, egg, milk, and cinnamon in a bowl.
4. Add the sweet potato. If the sweet potato makes the mixture too thick, add a bit more milk.
5. Add the peanut butter and blueberries and squash the blueberries.
6. Spray a pan with oil.
7. Scoop the pancake mixture into the pan and then flip.
