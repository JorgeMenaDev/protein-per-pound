## Instructions

1. Put the frozen chicken in a slow cooker with 1 cup of water, add spices (paprika, salt, pepper), leave it there for 2 hours aprox
2. at 2 hours, add the broccoli and cream, reduce water if its too much

## Notes

- I use frozen chicken but could be done also with fresh chicken it just require less time in the slow cooker
